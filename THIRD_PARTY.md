# Third-party software and assets

The custom noncommercial RimTube license does **not** restrict software or
materials received separately under their own licenses. In particular, mpv,
FFmpeg/ffprobe, yt-dlp, Node.js and their dependencies retain their respective
GPL/LGPL/BSD/MIT and other terms, including any commercial-use rights those terms
grant. Those tools are separate executables communicating with RimTube through
processes/IPC; their binaries are not stored in this Git repository.

The ready-to-play Windows bundle retains their original notices under
`Tools/licenses`, version/hash records under `Tools/bundled-tools.json`, and
provenance and direct source links under `Tools/provenance` and `Tools/Sources`.

Complete corresponding tool sources, dependencies, patches and build recipes are
public at the fixed Release:

https://github.com/as5611198/RimTube-ThirdParty-Sources/releases/tag/tools-20261102-0.2.0

Download its three source ZIPs and the metadata ZIP; GitHub's automatically
generated source ZIP is only the index repository. The source manifest and
SHA256SUMS identify exact assets. Keep each source Release available while its
matching binaries are distributed. Do not replace old version assets.

RimWorld and other game/mod assets belong to their owners. A developer must supply
their own legitimate game DLLs locally; this repository provides none. The
Gemini-assisted cover is identified in LICENSE-ASSETS.md; no exclusive rights in
AI-generated or third-party elements are asserted. Permission to use this player
does not confer rights to the audio/video sources played with it.
